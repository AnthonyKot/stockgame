# Scene review — clinical-axsm-2019

## Confirmed defects

- **ask_friend[3]** ("How much is already in the price?"): "The last close is $79.80, a 12-month high, up more than 2,500% over twelve months. Market capitalisation is about $2.75 billion with net cash of about $24 million [c11a]." Cited claim c11a only covers net cash (~$23.9M at 2019-09-30, derived from cash minus long-term debt); it contains no share price, no 12-month range, no return figure, and no market cap. player.json's own `missing_data` list states: "Market capitalization and enterprise value: missing, historical share price not in the evidence packet." The sentence states as fact a price, a 12-month-high status, a percentage return and a market cap that the case's own evidence packet says are absent. Minimal fix: drop the price/return/market-cap clause; keep only "Net cash is about $24 million on a rising share count [c11a]; this packet has no share price, so how much of a future approval is already priced in cannot be shown here."

- **ask_friend[2]** ("What else is due, and can they pay for it?") and **question**: "Four more readouts inside the next two quarters: the same drug in treatment-resistant depression and in Alzheimer's agitation, and a migraine drug in two trials [c7][c8]." The cited claim c8 itself lists five pending readouts, not four: STRIDE-1 (Q1 2020), ADVANCE-1 (H1 2020), AXS-07 MOMENTUM (Q4 2019), AXS-07 INTERCEPT (Q1 2020), and AXS-12 CONCERT — "the AXS-12 narcolepsy trial CONCERT had completed enrollment with topline data expected Q4 2019." The narcolepsy readout is due the same quarter as MOMENTUM and is simply dropped from the count. `question` repeats the same "four more readouts due." Minimal fix: "Five more readouts inside the next two quarters: the same drug in treatment-resistant depression and in Alzheimer's agitation, a migraine drug in two trials, and a narcolepsy drug [c7][c8]," and change `question`'s "four more readouts" to "five."

- **a4**: "After a 25x run the price already assumes approval; the risk is down." This packs a premise (a large prior run-up) and two separate conclusions ("already assumes approval," "the risk is down") into one assumption, contrary to the brief's rule that each assumption be a single testable proposition — "the risk is down" is a verdict, not something a later event tests. It also rests on the same unsourced run-up figure as ask_friend[3] above (no claim_ids attach to assumptions in this schema, and the only related figure in evidence, c11a, is net cash, not a price return). Minimal fix: split into a single proposition, e.g. "The price already reflects a large prior run-up in the stock" — and only once a sourced price figure exists; drop "the risk is down" as a bundled verdict.

## Unsupported or unverifiable

- **s1** claims-text: "second positive well-controlled trial in MDD" is cited only to c1, which reports GEMINI's topline result (16.6 vs 11.9 points, p=0.002) but never says it is the *second* such trial. The "second" framing exists only via c6 ("GEMINI, together with the previously completed ASCEND trial") and in player.json's own uncited narrative text, neither of which is in s1's `claim_ids`. Needs: add c6 to s1's claim_ids, or source a claim that itself states GEMINI is the second positive trial.

- **voice**: "its second pivotal trial" additionally assumes ASCEND was itself a *pivotal* trial, not just "previously completed" and "positive." The evidence packet never characterizes ASCEND's phase or design — player.json's `missing_data` says "ASCEND trial's own effect-size, confidence-interval and p-value detail: not in this packet; referenced only via GEMINI release's description of it as a previously completed positive trial." Needs: a source (e.g., the original ASCEND topline announcement) confirming ASCEND was a Phase 3/pivotal trial before "second pivotal" can stand as written.

## Editorial preferences (not defects)

- Voice's "enough to file next year" is looser than s3's cited "second half of 2020" [c6]; the two are consistent, but a reader could take "next year" as any time in 2020 rather than specifically H2.
- `question`'s "cash for about four quarters" rounds down the cited "roughly 4.5 quarters" [c11c]; "about four and a half quarters" would track the source more closely.
- ask_friend[1] names the trial "GEMINI" explicitly. This isn't inconsistent with the rest of the packet — player.json's own `sector_evidence` and `catalysts` sections already use the unmasked trial name — but if the masking policy is meant to extend beyond company name/ticker to trial identifiers, this is worth a second look at the policy level, not just this scene.
