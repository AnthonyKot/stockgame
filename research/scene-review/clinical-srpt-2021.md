# Scene review: clinical-srpt-2021 (Sarepta Therapeutics, cutoff 2021-01-08T09:00 ET)

## Confirmed defects

- **ask_friend[2].a** ("Is it expensive?"): "The last close is $168.95, up 30% over twelve months and 5% below the 12-month high... Market capitalisation is about $13.3 billion; net cash about $1.4 billion; enterprise value roughly 31 times 2019 revenue [c16]." — cited to c16 and c12, but c16 is "Full-year 2019 revenue was $380.8 million" and c12 is "cash, cash equivalents and investments were approximately $1.8 billion as of September 30, 2020"; neither contains a share price, 12-month range, market cap, net-cash, or EV figure. `player.json.financial_snapshot` marks this exact metric `"value": null, "status": "missing"`, stating "Historical share price is not in the eligible evidence packet, so market capitalization and enterprise value cannot be calculated," and `missing_data` repeats this. Wrong: fabricates precise, unsourced price/valuation numbers the packet explicitly says it doesn't have (and a specific, googleable price raises recognition risk). Fix: "Share price and market value are not established in this record; cash and investments are about $1.8 billion against $380.8 million of 2019 revenue [c12][c16]."

- **s1**: "primary functional endpoint not significant (p=0.37)," cited to `["c1","c2"]`. c1 is trial design only ("41 participants...4-7 years old"); c2 is the *biological* endpoint result ("28.1% mean expression (P<0.0001)"), a different endpoint with the opposite significance outcome. The actual source is c3: "Change in NSAA total score: Not statistically significant (P=0.37)." Wrong: cited claims don't contain the stated result. Fix: change claim_ids to `["c3"]`.

- **s2**: "primary biological endpoint met (p<0.0001) and the 4-5 year subgroup significant," cited to `["c3","c4"]`. c3 is actually the functional-miss claim above (p=0.37, not p<0.0001); c2 is the correct biological-endpoint source. Wrong: appears swapped with s1's citation. Fix: change claim_ids to `["c2","c4"]`.

- **s3**: "two approved drugs generate $121 million a quarter and cash is about $1.8 billion," cited to `["c9","c11"]`. c9 reads "Study 101 was an open-label, uncontrolled study of four ambulatory boys...mean 7.0-point improvement on the NSAA...at two years post-infusion" — unrelated to revenue or cash. The $1.8 billion figure's actual source, c12 ("approximately $1.8 billion in cash, cash equivalents and investments as of September 30, 2020"), is uncited. Wrong: one cited claim is irrelevant, and the claim that supports half the sentence is missing. Fix: change claim_ids to `["c11","c12"]`.

- **assumptions[a2]**: "The functional miss means the therapy does not work; the market is right to sell." The cutoff is 2021-01-08T09:00 ET, 30 minutes before the regular session opens on the first trading day after the 4:05pm Jan 7 release; no share-price or trading data appears anywhere in evidence.json or player.json (missing_data: "Market capitalization... missing, historical share price not in the evidence packet"). Wrong: states as settled that "the market" has already sold and been vindicated, when no market session has occurred yet within the cited record — a point-in-time error — and it bakes a verdict into what should be a testable proposition (also compound: efficacy judgment + market-behavior verdict). Fix: split into "The functional miss means the therapy does not work" (drop the market clause; no evidence of any post-announcement price action exists at the cutoff).

- **assumptions[a1]**: "The younger-patient subgroup result is real and will convince the regulator." Compound: bundles a scientific proposition ("the result is real/reproducible") with a prediction about a distinct institutional decision ("will convince the regulator"). These can diverge — and, per the source record's own age-4/8 vs younger-subgroup structure, a regulator could act on the biological endpoint alone without the subgroup result being reproducible. Fix: split into two — "The 4-5 year subgroup's functional benefit is a real, reproducible drug effect" and "Regulators will accept the biomarker/subgroup evidence as a basis for approval."

## Unsupported or unverifiable

- **voice**: "in first placebo-controlled trial" — voice carries no claim_ids. No evidence claim states this is the *first* placebo-controlled trial of the gene therapy; it's only inferable by contrast with the open-label, uncontrolled Study 101 (c9). Needed: an explicit statement (or a search confirming no earlier randomized/placebo-controlled trial of this program exists).
- **voice**: "company points to protein expression and a younger-patient subgroup" — uncited; the nearest source, c7, says management blamed "an imbalance between treatment arms" for the miss and describes a follow-on trial, but does not itself frame protein expression/subgroup as the company's rebuttal. Needed: a direct quote from the release characterizing management's response that way, beyond the biological (c2) and subgroup (c4) results existing on their own.

## Editorial preferences (not defects)

- **question**: "a setback to buy, or the first sign the lead program does not work" pairs an action-framed bull option ("to buy") against a plain bear one ("does not work"); a symmetric pairing (e.g., "a setback the market will look past, or the first sign...") would read more neutrally.
- **ask_friend[0].a** / player.json say "boys"; c1's excerpt says "children." Harmless given Duchenne muscular dystrophy is an X-linked disease that is essentially exclusive to males, but strictly the cited excerpt doesn't use that word.
- **assumptions[a3]**: "a setback, not an existential event" bundles two framings of one severity judgment; borderline compound but doesn't create divergent testability like a1/a2, so left as-is.
